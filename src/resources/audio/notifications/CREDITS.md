# Notification sound credits

The notification tones `ton-1.mp3` … `ton-12.mp3` in this folder are taken from
the **react-sounds** sound library and self-hosted here (we do not load them from
the react-sounds CDN at runtime, to keep the app free of third-party requests).

- **Source:** react-sounds — https://github.com/e3ntity/react-sounds (mirror:
  https://github.com/aediliclabs/react-sounds), catalogue: https://www.reactsounds.com/sounds
- **License:** MIT © Lukas Schneider. Full text: https://github.com/e3ntity/react-sounds/blob/master/LICENSE
- **Retrieved with:** `npx react-sounds-cli pick <name> --output=…` (offline/self-host mode)

MIT permits use and redistribution provided this copyright + licence notice is
kept — which this file satisfies.
